Scoring

Throw six dice and set aside at least one. You may set aside dice that don't score yet, to build towards a straight or three of a kind. Then throw the rest again, or bank what your set-aside dice are worth. A throw that doesn't raise that value is a bust and the turn's points are lost. If all six set-aside dice score, you bank them into the turn and throw all six again.
